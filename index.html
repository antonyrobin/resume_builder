What is a Reverse-Chronological Resume?

The reverse-chronological resume is the most widely used and recruiter-preferred resume format. It lists your work experience starting with your most recent position, making it easy for hiring managers and ATS systems to quickly assess your career progression. Our ATS Reverse-Chronological template uses a clean, single-column layout with system fonts for maximum compatibility.

What is a reverse-chronological resume? ▼

A reverse-chronological resume lists your work experience starting with your most recent position and working backward. It is the most popular resume format preferred by 90%+ of recruiters and ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) because it clearly shows career progression and makes it easy to identify relevant experience.

How do I create an ATS-friendly resume? ▼

To create an ATS-friendly resume: use a single-column layout, standard fonts (Arial, Helvetica), and avoid graphics, icons, and complex formatting. Use standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills). Our ATS Reverse-Chronological, ATS Functional, and ATS Hybrid templates handle all of this automatically.

What resume format should I use? ▼

For most job seekers, the reverse-chronological format is best — it's what recruiters expect. If you're changing careers or have employment gaps, try the functional/skills-based format to emphasize capabilities. The hybrid format is ideal for experienced professionals who want to highlight both skills and a strong work history.
